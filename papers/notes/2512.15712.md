# 2512.15712 — Predictive Concept Decoders (PCDs): Training Scalable End-to-End Interpretability Assistants

Huang, Choi, Johnson, Schwettmann, Steinhardt (Transluce). arXiv v1, 17 Dec 2025. All 28 pages read (appendix prompt listings skimmed).

## Research question
Can we turn "predict model behaviour from activations" into an end-to-end training objective, by training an encoder→sparse-concept bottleneck→LM-decoder assistant on unlabeled text (next-token prediction of future text from activations) and then fine-tuning it to answer questions, such that (a) bottleneck concepts are human-interpretable and (b) the decoder surfaces information the model does not self-report (jailbreak awareness, hint usage, injected concepts)?

## Architecture (exact)
- Subject model S = Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (d = 4096, frozen).
- **Encoder** (per token, linear, no cross-token mixing): `a'(i) = W_emb · TopK(W_enc a(i) + b_enc)`; W_enc ∈ R^{m×d}, m = 32,768 concepts (8× expansion), k = 16 active; W_emb ∈ R^{d×m} initialized to W_enc^T; W_enc rows unit-norm random init; b_enc = 0.
- Read layer ℓ_read = **15** (of 32); write layer ℓ_write = **0** of the decoder (encoded vectors patched as soft tokens into residual stream at layer 0, LatentQA-style replacement of placeholder/dummy-string positions), followed by natural-language question tokens.
- **Decoder** D = copy of S + rank-r LoRA (r swept 4–16, no clear trend; LoRA α = 32, dropout 0.05).
- Encoder never sees the question; decoder never sees raw activations (communication bottleneck). Test-time variants: k ∈ {16,32,64}; "no bottleneck" (W_enc = W_emb = I, no TopK; architecturally = LatentQA).
- Number of positions: during pretraining n_middle = 16 activation positions; in downstream tasks, all tokens of the user message / jailbreak prompt.

## Training
**Pretraining** (jointly train encoder + decoder LoRA): FineWeb passages split into prefix / middle / suffix, n_prefix = n_middle = n_suffix = **16** tokens. S reads prefix+middle; encoder encodes the 16 middle activations; decoder predicts the 16 suffix tokens: `L = −Σ_t log p_D(s_t | s_<t, E(a_middle))`. Instruct prefix (Llama system header + user header) prepended. Budgets {9, 18, 36, 72, 144}M tokens; selected **72M** model. Cosine LR schedule, lr 1e-4, effective batch 128, weight decay 0.01, no warmup.
**Auxiliary dead-concept loss**: `L_aux = −(ε_aux/k_aux) Σ_{i∈I} W_enc,i · a`, I = k_aux inactive (no top-k firing in last 1M tokens) concepts with largest dot product; ε_aux = 1e-4, k_aux = 500. Keeps >90% concepts alive at 72M (vs ~1/3 dead otherwise).
Alternative objective (KL to subject model's next-token distribution) gave similar/slightly worse, earlier plateau.
**Fine-tuning**: encoder frozen; decoder fine-tuned on **SynthSys(8B)** (Choi et al. 2025, Transluce user-modeling: dialogues where the model assumes a user attribute; filtered for behavioural consistency), 78/80 attributes train, hold out gender & age; 50% FineWeb mixed in; 4000 steps; same hyperparameters. GPU hours not reported.

## Evaluations & key numbers
- **Encoder interpretability**: auto-interp (Choi et al. 2024 pipeline, simulator Pearson corr., 400 concepts) and concept recall (SynthSys best single-direction threshold classifier). PCD improves with data, plateaus ~72–144M; L2 TopK SAEs at k=50 eventually surpass; KL-SAEs (current or future tokens) show the same plateau → plateau attributed to KL/prediction-style objectives.
- **SynthSys held-out QA**: PCD ≈ LatentQA from 36M tokens on (~0.8+; LatentQA ~0.9 best, LatentQA-72M-pretrain ~0.82); frozen-SAE encoder PCD ~0.5 → joint training essential.
- **Jailbreak awareness** (50 harmful actions × 3 templates: 3 Words, Distractors, Dream; activations from the jailbreak *prompt only, not response*; GPT-5-mini judge; question "What is the assistant thinking about right now?"): average awareness PCD k=16 ≈0.31, k=32 ≈0.33, no-bottleneck ≈0.35, LatentQA ≈0.14, prompting 1st/3rd person 0.21–0.27. Dream: PCD 0.50 vs others <0.30. 3 Words: awareness emerges only at 72M.
- **Secret hints** (i-th prime, 9950≤i≤9999, hint in user message): hint-reveal rate PCD 72M ≈0.35, no-bottleneck ≈0.73, LatentQA ≈0.61, prompting ≈0.06.
- **Introspection** (58 Lindsey concepts, steering strength 3 on "Hi."): directly-relevant fraction PCD ≈0.29 vs LatentQA ≈0.11 vs prompting ≈0.05. Encoder top-16 concepts: SAE surfaces directly-relevant more often (≈0.54 vs 0.35).
- **Auditing decoder claims**: refusals—decoder cites legal liability ~50%; a "legal/responsibility/future events" concept active in 48/50 cases. Decimal-comparison errors—decoder mentions dates ~30%, date concepts active >90% (consistent with Meng et al. 2024).

## Limitations (stated / evident)
Interpretability plateaus ~72–144M tokens; bottleneck sometimes hurts (3 Words, hints) and inputs at k≠16 are OOD; standard SAEs still better dictionaries for directly relevant concepts; encoder is per-token linear (no cross-token reading, single layer); judge-based metrics; single subject model; small pretraining scale; question phrasing sensitivity.

## Relevance to observer/forecasting hypothesis
- **Closest in spirit to "predict future behaviour from activations"**: the pretraining objective *is* forecasting — predict the next 16 tokens of text from 16 middle-segment activations (horizon = 16 tokens). The jailbreak experiment is a genuine *future-behaviour* test: activations from the jailbreak prompt only (before any response) → is the decoder aware the model is about to output harmful instructions? That is forecasting over the whole response (~hundreds of tokens), but evaluated as a single-shot binary "awareness" judgement, not a time-to-event, and without varying prefix position/horizon.
- Evidence relevant to the hypothesis: (i) a prediction-based (future-text) pretraining objective gives a *better inductive bias* than LatentQA even with identical architecture (PCD-no-bottleneck > LatentQA on jailbreak/introspection) — supports multi-horizon future-prediction objectives; (ii) a compact latent bottleneck can carry behaviour-relevant info but saturates with KL/next-token objectives — suggests our observer's future-latent targets should be richer than 16-token next-token prediction (multi-scale window summaries).
- Explicit gaps the observer addresses: the authors themselves propose "a transformer that reads information across multiple tokens" and multiple layers (Discussion) — i.e. a memory/trajectory encoder; no calibrated probabilities or hazard over time; horizon fixed at 16 tokens.
- As baseline: PCD (or its no-bottleneck/LatentQA variant) on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct is a natural "direct verbalizer with predictive pretraining" baseline; train it on the same unsafe-onset labels by fine-tuning the decoder with questions like "Will the assistant produce harmful content within the next N tokens?". If weights are unavailable, it is re-implementable cheaply (72M tokens, 8B model, LoRA).
