# 1807.03748 — Representation Learning with Contrastive Predictive Coding (CPC) (van den Oord, Li, Vinyals; DeepMind 2018)

## Research question
Can a universal unsupervised objective — predicting the *future in latent space* with a contrastive loss — learn representations that capture slow, high-level, long-range shared information across modalities?

## Method / architecture
- Encoder z_t = g_enc(x_t) (possibly lower temporal resolution); autoregressive context c_t = g_ar(z_{<=t}) (GRU in experiments).
- Do not model p(x_{t+k}|c_t) generatively; instead model the density ratio f_k(x_{t+k}, c_t) ∝ p(x_{t+k}|c_t)/p(x_{t+k}) (Eq. 2) with a log-bilinear score:
  f_k(x_{t+k}, c_t) = exp( z_{t+k}^T W_k c_t ) (Eq. 3), separate W_k per horizon k (non-linear/RNN predictors also allowed).
- Either z_t or c_t can be used downstream; c_t when past context is needed; pool over positions for sequence-level tasks.

## InfoNCE loss (exact)
Given X = {x_1,...,x_N} with one positive from p(x_{t+k}|c_t) and N−1 negatives from p(x_{t+k}):

  L_N = − E_X [ log( f_k(x_{t+k}, c_t) / Σ_{x_j ∈ X} f_k(x_j, c_t) ) ]   (Eq. 4)

- It is the categorical cross-entropy of identifying the positive; optimal f_k ∝ p(x_{t+k}|c_t)/p(x_{t+k}), independent of N (Eq. 5).
- MI bound: I(x_{t+k}; c_t) ≥ log(N) − L_N, tighter for larger N (App. A.1, Eqs. 6-11).
- Relation to MINE: with f = e^F, InfoNCE lower-bounds the MINE estimator up to a constant (Eqs. 12-15); MINE was equivalent on non-trivial tasks but unstable when the target is easy to predict (e.g., 1-step).
- Loss summed over horizons k = 1..K (K=12 audio, up to 5 rows images, 3 sentences text, 30 steps RL).

## Models / datasets / setups
- Audio: LibriSpeech 100h subset, 251 speakers; Kaldi force-aligned phones (41 classes); strided CNN encoder (5 layers, strides [5,4,2,2,2], 512 units; 10 ms per step), GRU 256; predict 12 steps; windows of 20,480 samples; Adam 2e-4; 8 GPUs × 8; ~300k updates. Labels/splits on Google Drive (link in paper).
- Vision: ImageNet ILSVRC; 7×7 grid of 64×64 crops (32 px overlap), ResNet-v2-101 encoder (no BN), mean-pooled 1024-d per patch; PixelCNN-style AR predicts up to 5 rows ahead.
- Text: BookCorpus; sentence encoder (1D conv + ReLU + mean-pool, 2400-d), GRU 2400; predict up to 3 future sentence embeddings; eval on MR, CR, Subj, MPQA, TREC with logistic regression.
- RL: DeepMind Lab 5 tasks, A2C + auxiliary CPC loss, predict up to 30 steps.

## Probe design
Linear classifier (logistic regression) on c_t (audio) or mean-pooled features (images); single hidden layer MLP probe as comparison.

## Metrics
Linear-probe accuracy; contrastive-task accuracy (positive logit ranked above negatives) vs horizon.

## Key numbers
- Phone classification: CPC 64.6 (linear) vs MFCC 39.7, random 27.6, supervised 74.6; 1-hidden-layer probe 72.5. Speaker ID: CPC 97.4 vs supervised 98.5.
- **Horizon ablation (#steps predicted)**: 2 → 28.5, 4 → 57.6, 8 → 63.6, 12 → 64.6, 16 → 63.8 — predicting further ahead yields better (slow) features, saturating ~12 steps.
- **Contrastive accuracy decays with horizon** (Fig. 3): ~1.0 at k=1, ~0.6 at k=3, ~0.2 at k=6, ~0.1 by k≈10, ~0.07 at k=20 (200 ms).
- Negatives: mixed speaker 64.6; same speaker 65.5; excluding current sequence 57.3/64.6; current sequence only 65.2.
- ImageNet linear top-1 48.7 (prev best 39.6), top-5 73.6. Text: MR 76.9, CR 80.1, Subj 91.2, MPQA 87.7, TREC 96.8. RL: improves 4/5 tasks (no effect on reactive lasertag).

## Limitations
Fixed horizons; linear W_k predictors; negatives from the minibatch; window/context size strongly affects performance (audio context ~1 s); NLP transfer results modest.

## Relevance to observer/forecasting hypothesis
- Canonical recipe for the observer's multi-horizon future-latent objective: encode future window summaries z_{t+k} (e.g., mean-pooled LLM activations over a future window), context c_t from a recurrent/attention memory over the past trajectory, per-horizon W_k, InfoNCE over in-batch negatives. Avoids reconstructing high-dimensional activations (MSE) and focuses capacity on predictable "slow features" (topic, intent, unsafe turn).
- Horizon ablation shows that training on longer horizons improves representation quality even though per-horizon accuracy falls — supports multi-scale targets for long lead distances.
- Use log(N) − L_N as an MI-lower-bound diagnostic per horizon to quantify "how much future information" the observer state carries vs a single-state probe, as a function of lead distance.
- Negative-sampling choice matters (same-sequence vs other-sequence negatives) — for LLM trajectories, other-sequence negatives test semantic forecasting; same-sequence negatives test temporal localization.
